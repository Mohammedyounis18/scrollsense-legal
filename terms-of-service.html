What ScrollSense does

ScrollSense helps you notice fast, mindless scrolling and stick to the focus times you plan. Depending on your phone and the settings you choose, it can turn apps grey, pause them, ask a quick question before an app opens, stop sound for a moment and open another app instead.

ScrollSense is a self-help tool. It is not medical treatment or advice, parental control software or a guaranteed blocker. Phone makers, system updates, battery settings and changes to other apps can affect how it works, so check that it's working before you rely on it.
