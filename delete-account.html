Delete your account

You can delete your account in the app in under a minute. If you can't open the app, email us and we'll do it for you.

Last updated 7 November 2026.

In the app

The steps are the same on Android and iPhone.

  1. Open ScrollSense.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll down to Account management.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Google or Apple may ask you to sign in again first, to make sure it's you.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or on iPhone in Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

Without the app

Email us from the address you signed in with, with the subject ScrollSense account deletion. Don't send a password or ID. We'll delete the account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

Email a deletion request

scrollsense.support@gmail.com

What gets deleted

  • Your ScrollSense sign-in account (your name, email, profile picture and account ID in Firebase Authentication).
  • Everything ScrollSense stored on that phone: focus plans, settings, history, attention span stats and cached weather.

What stays

  • Purchase records kept by Google Play, Apple and RevenueCat, which the law requires for tax, billing and fraud prevention.
  • Crash reports on Android, which aren't linked to your account and are deleted by Firebase Crashlytics after 90 days.
  • Data on another phone you used. Delete the account or use Reset local data there too, or uninstall the app.
